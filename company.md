      <div class="story__content">
        <span class="story__eyebrow">Who We Are</span>
        <h2>A Brewery Built on Passion</h2>
        <p>
          Amazing Beer Co. is an independent craft brewery based in Portland, Oregon.
          We were founded on a single, stubbornly simple belief: that the best beer is
          made by people who genuinely care about what goes into the glass.
        </p>
        <p>
          Today we brew three core beers — a lager, a stout, and an IPA — each
          crafted in small batches using premium ingredients and traditional techniques.
          We haven't grown so big that we've forgotten why we started, and we never plan to.
        </p>
        <p>
          We are proud to be independent, employee-owned, and answerable only to
          the people who drink our beer.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

<!-- ========================================================
     TIMELINE / HISTORY
     ======================================================== -->
<section class="section section--muted" aria-label="Company history">
  <div class="container">
    <header class="section-header">
      <span class="section-header__eyebrow">How We Got Here</span>
      <h2>The Amazing Beer Journey</h2>
      <p>
        From a rented garage to a nationally recognised craft brewery — here's
        how Amazing Beer Co. grew from a weekend hobby into something much bigger.
      </p>
    </header>
    <div class="timeline" role="list">
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2010</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>The Garage Years Begin</h4>
          <p>
            Co-founders Marcus Erikson and Sarah Jensen brew their first batch in
            Marcus's garage on a cold November weekend. The batch — a straightforward
            pale ale — is shared with neighbours. Every bottle disappears before midnight.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2012</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>First Commercial Batch</h4>
          <p>
            Amazing Beer Co. is officially incorporated. The first commercial batch of
            Golden Horizon Lager rolls out to ten local bars and restaurants. The waiting
            list grows faster than the team can brew.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2014</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>Midnight Stout &amp; Hop Odyssey IPA Launch</h4>
          <p>
            Head of Flavour Innovation Aiko Tanaka joins the team and immediately
            sets to work on two ambitious new recipes. Midnight Stout and Hop Odyssey
            IPA both debut to a sold-out taproom launch event.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2017</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>Pacific Northwest Distribution</h4>
          <p>
            A distribution deal covering Oregon, Washington, and Idaho means Amazing
            Beer reaches shelves and taps across the Pacific Northwest for the first time.
            The team doubles in size within twelve months.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2019</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>National Recognition</h4>
          <p>
            Golden Horizon Lager wins Gold at the US Craft Beer Championships.
            Midnight Stout takes Silver in the same year. The team celebrates
            with a very long, very satisfying evening in the taproom.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="timeline-item" role="listitem">
        <div class="timeline-item__year">
          <span class="year-num">2023</span>
        </div>
        <div class="timeline-item__content">
          <h4>New Brewing Facility Opens</h4>
          <p>
            We move into a purpose-built, state-of-the-art brewing facility on the
            banks of the Willamette River. The new space triples our brewing capacity
            while keeping every batch hand-crafted by our award-winning team.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- ========================================================
     OUR VALUES
     ======================================================== -->
<section class="section section--dark" aria-label="Our values">
  <div class="container">
    <header class="section-header">
      <span class="section-header__eyebrow">What Drives Us</span>
      <h2>Our Values</h2>
      <p>
        Four principles that guide every decision we make — from which hops
        to buy to how we treat our team and our community.
      </p>
    </header>
    <div class="values-grid">
      <div class="value-card">
        <h3>🌾 Quality Without Compromise</h3>
        <p>
          We source the finest ingredients from trusted growers and refuse to
          substitute quality for cost. Every batch is tested rigorously before
          it ever reaches a glass.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="value-card">
        <h3>🤝 Community First</h3>
        <p>
          Beer is about bringing people together. We sponsor local events,
          support independent venues, and give a portion of every sale
          to Portland community organisations.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="value-card">
        <h3>🌍 Sustainability Matters</h3>
        <p>
          Our new brewery runs on 100% renewable energy. Spent grain goes to
          local farms. Water usage is monitored and minimised. We're always
          looking for ways to do better.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="value-card">
        <h3>🎨 Constant Curiosity</h3>
        <p>
          We never stop experimenting. Our small-batch seasonal programme gives
          our brewers the freedom to explore new styles, ingredients, and
          techniques — because great beer is always evolving.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
